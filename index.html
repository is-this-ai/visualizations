Welcome to the companion website for our research on AI-generated music detection. This site provides interactive visualizations and experimental results from our study on the robustness of AI music detection systems.

Interactive Visualizations

UMAP Projections

Explore interactive two-dimensional and three-dimensional UMAP projections of our dataset. These visualizations reveal the underlying structure and relationships in the audio feature space, allowing you to investigate how AI-generated and human-composed music samples cluster in high-dimensional space.

Audio Analysis Results

View detailed experimental results examining how the IRCAM Amplify detector responds to various audio transformations. This analysis investigates the impact of filtering transformations and sampling rate conversions on AI detection performance, using a randomly selected subset of five samples per source from our test set.
